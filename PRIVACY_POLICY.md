Memobun ("Memobun," "we," "us," or "our"), operated by Sophia Lin, provides a
cozy study‑companion mobile app (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what
information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you
have.

If you have questions, contact us at **memobunsupport@gmail.com**.

---

## 1. Who this applies to
The App is intended for users **13 years of age and older**. It is not directed
to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from
children under 13. See **Children's Privacy** below.

## 2. Information we collect

We collect what the App needs to work, plus the limited analytics and advertising
data described in **(g)** and **(h)** below. We do **not** sell your personal
information.

**a. Account information**
- Your **email address** when you create an account or sign in with email and
  password.
- If you choose **"Continue with Google,"** basic sign‑in information from Google
  (such as your email) used to create/authenticate your account.

**b. Profile information (shared with friends you add)**
- **Display name**, a generated **friend code**, an optional **birthday**, an
  optional **profile description**, your chosen **companion / outfit / avatar
  frame**, and **study statistics** (e.g., current/longest streak, total minutes
  studied). Your profile card can be viewed by other users who have your friend
  code.

**c. Content you create**
- **Direct messages** you send to friends within the App.
- **Tasks, exams, mood entries, and notes** you add to plan and track your
  studying.

**d. Usage & game data**
- Your in‑app progress and settings (e.g., coins, completed sessions, owned
  items, preferences, language). This is synced to our cloud so your progress
  follows your account across devices.

**e. Device information**
- A **randomly generated device identifier** we create to enforce a single active
  session per account (so your account is only used on one device at a time). We
  do **not** collect precise location, your contacts, photos, or
  microphone/camera data. For the advertising identifier, see **(h)** below.

**f. Notifications**
- If you enable study reminders, they are scheduled **locally on your device.**
  We do not operate a push‑notification server and do not collect push tokens.

**g. Usage analytics**
- We use **PostHog**, a third‑party analytics provider, to understand how the App
  is used. This covers in‑app events (for example completing a study session,
  opening a chat, or making a purchase), your device type and OS version, the App
  version, and an approximate location (country or city) derived from your IP
  address. Events are linked to your account ID when you are signed in; guests
  stay anonymous.

**h. Advertising**
- The App offers **optional** rewarded video ads (for example "Watch a video" in
  the coin shop), supplied by **Google AdMob**. If you choose to watch one, AdMob
  may receive your **device advertising identifier**, IP address, and
  ad‑interaction data such as whether the ad was viewed or completed.
- Ads are **non‑personalized by default.** Personalized advertising, which uses
  your advertising identifier, happens only where you have allowed it: on iOS we
  ask through the **App Tracking Transparency** prompt, and on Android it follows
  your Google advertising settings, which you can change or reset at any time in
  your device settings.
- The App is fully usable without watching any ads.

## 3. How we use information
We use the information above to:
- Create and secure your account and keep you signed in on one device at a time;
- Provide core features — sessions, tasks, exams, mood tracking, the shop, and
  cosmetic companions/outfits/frames;
- Sync your progress across your devices;
- Power social features (friend connections and direct messages);
- Send the local study reminders you set up;
- Show the optional rewarded video ads that let you earn extra coins;
- Understand how the App is used, and maintain, troubleshoot, and improve it.

We do **not** sell your personal information. We do not use your content —
messages, tasks, notes, or study data — for advertising; the only data used for
advertising is that described in **2(h)**.

## 4. How information is shared

**With other users:** your profile card (name, friend code, optional birthday/
description, chosen character, and study stats) is visible to people who have
your friend code, and your direct messages are delivered to the friends you send
them to.

**With service providers (sub‑processors)** who process data on our behalf to run
the App:

| Provider | Purpose | More info |
|---|---|---|
| **Supabase** | Account authentication, database, file storage, and realtime sync (profiles, progress, friends, messages) | https://supabase.com/privacy |
| **PostHog** | Product analytics as described in 2(g); processed on US servers | https://posthog.com/privacy |
| **Google AdMob** | Serves the optional rewarded video ads described in 2(h); processes advertising identifiers and ad‑interaction data | https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads |
| **RevenueCat** | Processes and validates in‑app purchases and subscription status | https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy |
| **Apple / Google** | Sign‑in (Google) and app distribution / in‑app purchases | https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy / https://policies.google.com/privacy |
| **Expo** | App build and runtime infrastructure | https://expo.dev/privacy |

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights,
safety, and security of our users or the App.

## 5. Data retention
We keep your account and associated data for as long as your account is active.
When you ask us to delete your account (see below), we delete your profile,
synced progress, friend connections, and messages associated with your account,
except where we must retain certain information to comply with legal obligations.

## 6. Your choices and rights
- **Access/update:** you can edit your profile and content in the App at any time.
- **Account & data deletion:** you can delete your account and associated data
  directly in the App (**Settings → Delete account**). You can also request
  deletion by emailing **memobunsupport@gmail.com** from your account email; we
  will process verified requests within a reasonable time.
- **Notifications:** you can disable reminders in the App or in your device
  settings.
- Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights (e.g., to access,
  correct, delete, or port your data, or to object to certain processing). To
  exercise these, contact **memobunsupport@gmail.com**.

## 7. Security
We use reputable infrastructure providers and reasonable technical and
organizational measures (including encrypted connections and access controls) to
protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure,
so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 8. International data transfers
Our providers may process and store data in countries other than where you live,
including the United States. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards
for such transfers.

## 9. Children's privacy
The App is intended for users **13 and older.** We do not knowingly collect
personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has
provided us personal information, contact **memobunsupport@gmail.com** and we will
delete it.

## 10. Changes to this policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will revise the "Last
updated" date above and, for material changes, provide a more prominent notice.
Continued use of the App after changes take effect means you accept the updated
policy.
