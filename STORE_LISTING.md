## Full description (both stores)

```
Make studying feel less lonely.

Memobun is a cozy study companion. Pick a character, start a focus session, and
they'll sit at the bakery desk and study right alongside you — so the hardest
part, actually starting, feels a little easier.

A SIMPLE LOOP THAT KEEPS YOU COMING BACK
Choose a duration, a subject, and how you're feeling. Focus. Finish, and bank a
Focus Coin for every minute you studied. Spend those coins on new companions,
outfits, rooms, and treats — then come back tomorrow and keep your streak alive.

Coins are only paid out when you finish a session. It's a small thing that makes
the last ten minutes much easier to sit through.

STUDY TOGETHER, LIVE
Open a multiplayer room and study in real time with friends. Every desk shows
that person's timer and topic, so you can feel the room working without anyone
saying a word. Prefer someone new? Study Buddy pairs you with a one-week
accountability partner, built safety-first.

MAKE IT YOURS
Collect companions, each with their own personality and wardrobe. Decorate your
bakery with new rooms and desks. Loop gentle ambient tracks — rain, ocean,
fireplace, night — or connect Spotify and bring your own focus soundtrack.

KEEP TRACK OF EVERYTHING
Tasks and to-dos. Exam countdowns. A subject manager. Log your mood at the start
of each session and watch it turn into weekly mood and subject reports, so you
can see when you actually study best.

STREAKS WITH A LITTLE GRACE
Build a daily study streak, and protect it with streak freezes when life gets in
the way. Missing one day shouldn't undo a month.

ACHIEVEMENTS
Milestones to chase as your habits grow — each one pays bonus coins.

MEMOBUN PLUS
An optional subscription for a little extra cozy: double coins, a higher daily
earning cap, custom timers and saved presets, every ambient sound unlocked,
Spotify and disco mode, advanced reports, more exams and subjects, monthly
streak freezes, a monthly room ticket, a gold avatar frame, and the exclusive
Golden Teahouse room — plus an outfit and room that stay yours forever, even if
Plus ends.

Memobun is free to use. Optional rewarded videos can earn you extra coins, and
you can play entirely without watching any.

Available in English, 日本語, 中文 (简体 & 繁體), 한국어, Español, and Français.

Made for ages 13+.
```
