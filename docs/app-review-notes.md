# App Review notes

Paste into **App Store Connect → the version → App Review Information → Notes**.
Reusable every submission; update only the bracketed demo account.

Keep it short — reviewers skim. Everything here either answers a question they will
ask anyway, or pre-empts a guideline this app has been rejected under before
(2.5.4 background audio, 2.3.6 age rating, 5.1.1(v) data collection).

---

Memobun is a study timer with a collectible companion character. You set a length,
study, and earn coins toward cosmetic items.

GETTING IN
Tap "Continue as guest" on the sign-in screen — no account needed, and the whole
solo experience (timer, subjects, calendar, shop, wardrobe) is available that way.
To review the social features, sign in with:
    [demo Apple/Google or email account — fill in before submitting]
    [password]

AGE
The app is 13+. On first launch it asks the user to affirm they meet that minimum.
No date of birth is collected or stored — the previous date-of-birth wheel was
removed in response to guideline 5.1.1(v). The optional birthday in Settings is a
month and day only, used for an in-app gift.

ACCOUNT DELETION
Settings → Account → "Delete account". Deletes the account and its cloud data from
within the app, no email or web form required.

SOCIAL FEATURES
Friend requests must be accepted before anyone is connected. Users can block and
report, and message text is filtered. Study rooms are private and joined by invite.

PURCHASES
Coin packs (consumable) and Memobun Plus (auto-renewing subscription), both via
StoreKit and testable in the sandbox. Nothing is required to use the app.

OPTIONAL EXTRAS
- Rewarded video ads in the coin shop are entirely optional (user taps to watch).
- Spotify control needs the reviewer's own Spotify Premium account. The app is
  fully functional without connecting it.

BACKGROUND AUDIO
The app declares no background audio mode and plays nothing while backgrounded.
