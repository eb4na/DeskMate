# Memobun 1.1.4 — What's New

## English (U.S.)
• Import your syllabus: upload a PDF, snap a photo or paste the text, and Memobun finds your exams and due dates. Everything is read on your phone.
• Custom timer lengths and multiple study reminders are now free for everyone
• Move a task to another day, even just one day of a repeating task
• Delete exams you no longer need
• Task reminders now show your task's title and notes
• Finished tasks move to the bottom of your list
• Every outfit is animated again
• Smaller fixes and polish
