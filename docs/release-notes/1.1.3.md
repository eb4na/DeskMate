# Memobun 1.1.3 — What's New

One block per App Store Connect localization. French (Canada) reuses French.

---

## English (U.S.)

Need a little longer?
Tap the pink + next to End session to add more time without stopping.

Meet Meruru and Gray
A dreamy unicorn who talks soft and slow, and a very serious chef who is trying very hard to look cool.

Companions that move
Lots of companions and outfits are now animated, from sleepy blinks to a boxing bounce.

Disco mode for everyone
Turn your study room into a little dance floor, no Plus needed.

Also new: tap the "i" on a companion card to read about their outfit, a To-Do List view for tasks, repeating tasks you can tick off one day at a time, a fresh new app icon, and lots of fixes and polish.

## Chinese (Simplified)

会动的伙伴
