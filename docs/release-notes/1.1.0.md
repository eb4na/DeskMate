# Memobun 1.1.0 — What's New

Release notes for App Store Connect. One block per listing locale — copy the text
under each heading into the matching "What's New in This Version" field.

App Store Connect lists 9 localizations for this app. The app's own UI ships in 7
languages (en, zh, zh-Hant, ja, ko, es, fr) — **Arabic is not one of them**, so an
Arabic listing sends people to a UI they can't read. See the note at the bottom.

---

## English (U.S.)

Lock in
A new focus mode: leave the app and the session ends, but see it through and earn double coins.

Your time, by subject
Progress is now a study-time tracker. See how your hours split across subjects — this week, this month, this year, or all time. Your full history is free for everyone.

A notebook for your subjects
Subjects now live on a handwritten ruled page, and each one can have its own colour.

A calendar you can mark up
Give any day its own shape and colour, and see what's due at a glance.

See your companions properly
Companions and recipes are shown one at a time, large enough to actually enjoy the artwork.

Also: leaving the app no longer drops you out of a shared study room, plus a batch of fixes and polish.

## Note on the Arabic listing

The app has no Arabic UI. `src/i18n/` ships en, zh, zh-Hant, ja, ko, es and fr only,
and the language picker offers those seven. An Arabic App Store listing therefore
sends Arabic speakers to an app that falls back to another language, and it is also
laid out left-to-right with no RTL support.

Two ways out, both in App Store Connect rather than in a build:

1. **Remove the Arabic localization from the listing** — the cleanest fix while the
   app has no Arabic UI.
2. **Keep it** and accept that the store page is translated but the app is not.

Worth deciding before submitting; a store listing promising Arabic is a common source
of one-star "not in my language" reviews.
