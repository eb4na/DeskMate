What's inside

Everything that makes studying feel sweet

A whole cozy world built around one simple loop: study, earn, unlock, and come back tomorrow.

Cozy study sessions

Your companion sits at the bakery desk and studies with you. Pick a duration, subject, and mood, then focus together.

Earn Focus Coins

Bank a coin for every minute — but only when you finish. Spend them on companions, outfits, rooms, and treats.

Streaks & freezes

Build a daily study streak with a little grace for missed days, and protect it with streak freezes.

Study together, live

Open a multiplayer room and study in real time with friends — each desk shows their timer and topic.

Study Buddy matching

Get paired with a one-week accountability buddy — a friendly, safety-first nudge to keep showing up.

Quests & achievements

Three fresh daily quests for bonus coins, plus achievements to chase as your habits grow.

Shop & customize

Decorate your bakery with new rooms and desks, and dress your companion in collectible outfits.

Study sounds & Spotify

Loop gentle ambient tracks, or hook up your own Spotify for the perfect focus soundtrack.

Tasks, exams & moods

Track to-dos and exam countdowns, log how you feel, and watch it all in weekly mood & subject reports.
