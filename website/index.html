Good to know

Frequently asked questions

A few things you might be wondering before Memobun opens its doors.

Is Memobun free?

Yes — Memobun is free to download and play, and you can unlock everything just by studying and earning coins. An optional Memobun Plus subscription and coin packs are there if you'd like a little extra.

What devices does it work on?

Memobun works on phones and tablets, with more platforms on the way.

When does it launch?

Very soon! Follow @memobun.app on Instagram to hear the moment it goes live.

Can I study with friends?

Absolutely. Study solo with your companion, open a multiplayer room to focus live with friends, or get matched with a friendly one-week study buddy.

Who is Memobun for?

Anyone who wants studying to feel a little cozier. Memobun is designed for ages 13 and up, with safety-first social features.
