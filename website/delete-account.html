Memobun — account and data deletion

You can delete your Memobun account and its data at any time, either from inside the app or by emailing us. Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone.

Delete your account in the app

This is the fastest way, and it takes effect immediately.

  1. Open Memobun and make sure you are signed in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Type your display name (or the word DELETE if you have not set one) to confirm.
  5. Confirm. Your account and data are erased right away and you are signed out.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot sign in or no longer have the app installed, email memobunsupport@gmail.com from the email address on the account, with the subject "Delete my account". We will verify the request and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

Deleting your account removes your account and the data linked to it from our systems, including:

  • your account and sign-in record, and your email address;
  • your profile, display name, and friend code;
  • your study history, streaks, coins, items, companions, and other in-app progress;
  • your subjects, tasks, and exam countdowns;
  • your friends list, friend requests, direct messages, and block list;
  • your date of birth.

If you used Memobun as a guest, no account was ever created and your data stayed only on your device — deleting the app (or clearing its data) removes it.

What we cannot delete

Purchase and subscription records are held by Apple and Google as the sellers of in-app purchases, under their own policies. We cannot remove those, and deleting your Memobun account does not cancel an active subscription — cancel that in your App Store or Google Play account.

We may keep limited records where the law requires it, for example transaction records needed for tax or accounting.

Deleting some of your data without deleting your account

You do not have to delete your whole account to remove data. In the app you can delete individual tasks, subjects, and exam countdowns, and you can remove friends at any time. If you would like other data removed while keeping your account, email memobunsupport@gmail.com and tell us what you would like deleted.

Contact us

Questions about deleting your account or data? Email memobunsupport@gmail.com. You can also read our Privacy Policy.
