## License quick-reference

- **CC0 / Public Domain** — no attribution needed; commercial use OK; edits OK.
- **CC BY** — attribution **required** (credit author + license); commercial + edits OK.
- **CC BY-SA** — attribution required; derivatives must share the same license.
- **CC BY-NC** — attribution required; **no commercial use** (don't use if the app monetizes).
- **CC BY-ND** — attribution required; **no edits/derivatives** (can't trim or loop).
- **Purchased / royalty-free "no attribution"** (Epidemic, Artlist, Uppbeat paid, etc.) — keep the purchase receipt/license file; no credit needed.

## Notes

- Freesound shows a "Get attribution text" link even on CC0 sounds — that's optional courtesy, not a requirement.
- CC0 sounds can later be deleted or relicensed by the uploader, so keeping a screenshot of the license panel at download time is a good extra safeguard.
