# Memobun — Character & Skin Dialogue Guide

Use this to generate in-character lines. **Memobun** is a cozy anime-bakery study-companion app: a cute character studies *with* the user at a desk. This guide lists every **character** (their fixed *voice*) and every **skin/outfit** (the *mood/flavor* on top of that voice).

**How to use it:** the **character = the voice** (how they talk, their personality, verbal tics). The **skin = the costume/mood** for that session — flavor the same voice with the outfit's vibe. So "Bunny in Jirai Kei" still talks like vain-princess Bunny, just with a moodier edge.

**What I need lines for (example):** short *pet/tap* reactions (1 sentence, said when the user taps the character on the home screen). But the voices below work for any line type (session start, encouragement, break, etc.). Keep lines short, cozy, and in-voice. No emojis.

---

## 1. Bun  *(the starter — a strawberry bun-bunny)*
**Voice:** Sweet, devoted, earnest. Gentle and a little shy. Loves the user openly. Uses soft "bun" puns now and then (e.g. "let's rise together," "warm from the oven"). Pure cinnamon-roll energy — never sarcastic.

**Skins:**
- **Strawberry** *(default)* — Warm, familiar, comforting. The everyday cozy Bun; "the friend you didn't know you were missing."
- **Angel** — Soft, gentle, quietly kind. Serene and reassuring; kindness that doesn't announce itself.
- **Berry Princess** — Sweet but a touch regal; golden, confident warmth. Wears a crown like it was always hers — gracious, not bratty.
- **Snow Rabbit** — Wintry, delicate, hopeful. Calm in the cold; "a flower that blooms against the frost." Quiet resilience.
- **Strawberry Dreams** — Sleepy, snug, nighttime. Lantern-low, soft all the way down. Drowsy bedtime coziness.

---

## 2. Cocoa  *(a cozy barista cat)*
**Voice:** Warm barista energy — "work hard now, treat yourself later." Uses coffee/tea/baking metaphors ("let it steep," "don't let your focus go cold"). Steady and encouraging, with a slightly cool, knowing edge. Present without trying too hard.

**Skins:**
- **Top Tier** *(default)* — Effortlessly warm and grounded; just *is*, no fuss. Reliable, mellow.
- **Relax** — Autumn-afternoon calm. Unhurried, slow-breathing, cozy. "The afternoon that forgets to end."
- **Demon** — Sharp, edgy, mischievous — but steady underneath. Playfully cocky; an edge that "knows exactly where it ends."

---

## 3. Bunny  *(a vain little princess rabbit)*
**Voice:** Vain, cute-bratty little princess. **Speaks in the third person** ("Bunny says…", "Bunny is always right"). Adorable and knows it; bossy but endearing. Demands attention, gives affection on her terms.

**Skins:**
- **Cutest Thing Ever** *(default)* — Peak adorable-and-knows-it. Sweet on the outside, fully aware of her power.
- **Jirai Kei** — Moodier, edgier, "menhera"-cute. Doesn't explain herself; sulky-stylish, lets the look do the talking.
- **Blue Peony** — Refined, elegant, soft-spoken royalty. "Speaks softly; doesn't have to speak twice." Quiet authority.

---

## 4. Miel  *(a sleepy honey bear)*
**Voice:** Drowsy, soft, half-asleep — but always shows up. "Nap now, study after." Yawns, trails off, talks about warmth and honey and naps. Sweet and a little mumbly; sleepy but caring.

**Skins:**
- **Honey Bear** *(default)* — Warm, welcoming, freshly-baked coziness. A hug that didn't wait to be asked for.
- **Champion** — Uncharacteristically focused and determined. Quietly competitive; "already there before the bell." (Fun contrast to the usual sleepiness.)
- **ZZZ** *(pajamas)* — Fully sleepy-mode. Pajamas, soft timers, gentle naps. Cozy, drowsy, "even in sleep, something here cares."

---

## 5. Tira  *(a deadpan dropout)*
**Voice:** Dry, deadpan, ironic. The "I dropped out, but *you* should study" type — lazy-cool mentor energy. Composed, unhurried, a little aloof; understated humor. Never gushy.

**Skins:**
- **Graceful Walk** *(default)* — Composed, deliberate, never in a hurry. Calm stillness that "already knows how this ends."
- **Choco Mint** — Sweet surface, steady core. A little softer/cuter while keeping the cool composure.
- **Sleepover** — Relaxed, night-in, unhurried. Long evenings and slow mornings; rest that means it.
- **Carefree Days** — Wandering, free, in-transit. Sunflowers past the train window; "the hours between nowhere and somewhere — the freest there are."

---

## 6. Hanji  *(a quiet lavender presence — earned, not bought)*
**Voice:** Quiet, mysterious, calm. Says little, means it. Reserved and gentle; a steady presence that "simply remains." Speaks in short, soft, slightly poetic lines. Rarely effusive.

**Skins:**
- **Quiet Lavender** *(default)* — Still, understated, ever-present. Arrived quietly and stayed; calm company.
- **Ivory Rose** — Dressed-up, hopeful, quietly faithful. "Dressed like the day mattered, before knowing what it would bring." A touch more open and tender.
